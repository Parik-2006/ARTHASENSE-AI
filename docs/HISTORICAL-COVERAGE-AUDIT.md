## 2. Five-year window, requirement 2 matrix

Window = the latest 60 calendar months ending at each indicator's newest stored month. **A–E are different quantities.**

| | CPI (Nov 2021 – Oct 2026) | WPI (Nov 2021 – Oct 2026) | IIP (Oct 2021 – Sep 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** calendar months in window | 60 | 60 | 60 |
| **B** months with an observation in any series | 60 | 60 | 60 |
| **C** months on **one** comparable series | **38** (CPI Back); 50 incl. † CPI 2012 | **54** (2011-12) | **54** (2011-12) |
| **D** months only reachable by spanning bases | 60 (Back + Current) | 60 (2011-12 + 2022-23) | 60 (2011-12 + 2022-23) |
| **E** forecast-eligible months (the series the forecaster trains on) | **22** (CPI Current) | **43** (WPI 2022-23) | **42** (IIP 2022-23) |
| Officially linked pair published by the source | **Yes: Back + Current = 60 months on base 2024** | No (factor documented, series not published linked) | No (same) |

Reading it honestly:

- **CPI:** a **60-month headline on base 2024 exists officially** (Back × official factor, then Current). It is *official but
  method-joined*: the join at Dec 2024 → Jan 2025 is a −1.20 % step (102.90 → 101.67) because the factor matches *annual*
  geometric means over 2025, not the boundary month. That is expected from the method, but it means a month-on-month change
  across the join is not a real price movement. ArthaSense keeps the two as separate segments, shows no change across the
  join, and does not forecast across it without your approval. CPI forecasting today sees only 22 months.
- **WPI and IIP:** **no single series covers 60 months.** The best is 54 months on the legacy base (stopped at source in
  Mar/Apr 2026); the six most recent months exist only on the new base. A 60-month view exists only by showing two series
  side by side, or by applying the documented factor at read time (an ArthaSense calculation, not a published index).
- **Forecast eligibility (E):** training uses the new-base series only (22 / 43 / 42 months). Longer history is available
  but would need a linked series, which is a methodological decision (see `docs/FORECAST-DATA-IMPACT.md`).

> **Note (2026-11-23):** of the −1.20 % join step, about −1.02 pp is the real Jan 2025 price fall and about −0.17 pp is
> the method. See `docs/OVERVIEW-CPI-IMPROVEMENTS-REPORT.md` §3 for this and for the CPI forecasting policy now used.
